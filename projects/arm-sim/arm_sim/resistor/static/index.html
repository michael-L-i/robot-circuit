Diagnose before replacing

Arduino 5 V → TP1 → 330 Ω resistor socket → TP2 → red LED → GND. A 100 kΩ pulldown makes an open output read 0 V.

TP1 at 5 V and TP2 at 0 V with a dark LED identify an open resistor. A missing supply must be fixed separately. After replacement, TP2 near 2 V and an illuminated LED verify the circuit. A null sample means failed probe contact, never zero volts.

MuJoCo contacts simulate grasping, probing and seating. Camera mode uses taught markers and calibrated poses; electrical sensing is idealized. Physical hardware requires fixture calibration and contact validation.
