From simulation to the bench

5 V → jumper → mesh → 330 Ω → LED → GND. The test pad shares the mesh input net; a 100 kΩ pulldown makes a disconnected input read 0 V.

MuJoCo mode runs the official Seeed reBot B601-RS model with its coupled parallel gripper: grasps are friction contacts, the probe reading and the closed circuit come from contacts between the probe tip, test pad, jumper pin and copper mesh. Breadboard and mesh retention are modelled as welds, and the electronics are ideal. Camera mode measures taught marker poses from rendered pixels. Optical flags need physical mounts; the probe sleeve and grasp offsets must be matched and calibrated on hardware. Neither backend validates real contact forces.
